02 / RESEARCH

Questions worth testing.

Research in knowledge graph reliability and cross-robot learning.

Accepted · Short paper CANDAR 2026 / WANC 2026

Per-Relation Reliability of LLM-Based Knowledge Graph Extraction: A Verification-First Study on Scientific Abstracts

Shandong He, Kenji Ono

A relation-level study of extraction reliability across 200 scientific abstracts, combining 360 human judgments, two LLM judges, and self-consistency analysis.

Data & reproduction code ↗ Accepted; proceedings forthcoming
Under review CoRL 2026 Workshop / Pretrain to Adapt 2026

Task-Dependent Benefits of Cross-Embodiment Pretraining: A Controlled Study of Geometric Demonstration Selection

Embodied learning · Cross-robot transfer

Controlled XArm6 → Panda transfer with Diffusion Policy in ManiSkill: two tasks, seven conditions, and three training seeds. Pretraining benefits depend on the task; tested geometric selectors do not consistently outperform random selection.
